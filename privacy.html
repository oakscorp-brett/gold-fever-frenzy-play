Gold Fever Frenzy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Gold Fever Frenzy is made by Brett Johnson. This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are used.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.
  • If optional in-app purchases are offered, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details.

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: contact Brett Johnson at brett@oakscorptreecare.com
