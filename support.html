Common questions

Is the game free?
Yes. Gold Fever Frenzy is free to play, with no ads. There is one optional purchase, the Prospector Starter Pack ($2.99 in the US, one per player), which shows exactly what you get. Nothing is random.

Where is the Starter Pack, and how do I restore it?
On the map, tap 🛒 Shop at the bottom of the screen. If you reinstall the game or get a new iPhone, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an account or internet?
No. The game works fully offline and doesn't need an account.

Where is my progress saved?
Your progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I turn off the sounds or the prospector's voice?
Tap the ⚙ button at the top of the screen. You can turn music, sound effects and the prospector's voice on or off there.

The flashing effects bother me.
Tap the ⚙ button at the top of the screen and turn on Reduce Flashing.
