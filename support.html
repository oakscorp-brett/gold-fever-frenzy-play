Common questions

Is the game free?
Yes. Gold Fever Frenzy is free to play, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account or internet?
No. The game works fully offline and doesn't need an account.

Where is my progress saved?
Your progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I turn off the sounds or the prospector's voice?
Use the 🔊 button for sound effects, the 🎵 button for music, and the 🗣️ button for the prospector's voice.

The flashing effects bother me.
Tap the ✨ button at the top of the screen to reduce flashing.
